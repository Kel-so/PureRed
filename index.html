My Journey

CRAFTING STORIES AT HIGH SPEED.

I'm a video editor and filmmaker focused on modern, dynamic narratives that instantly capture the viewer's attention. My influences come directly from independent cinema, urban culture, streetwear and contemporary aesthetics.

I work transforming raw footage into communication pieces with aggressive rhythm, impeccable sound design and eye-popping color grading. Whether it's a 30-second eSports teaser or a 10-minute post-event documentary, the goal is the same: to give goosebumps and hold attention.
