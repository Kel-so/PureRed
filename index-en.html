Behind the edits

KELSON BUDIN

Video editor and motion designer creating high-energy content for gaming, events and brands.

My job is turning raw footage into videos that hold attention from start to finish: aggressive pacing when the content calls for speed, breathing room when the story needs weight. Sound design, color grading and motion are part of every delivery — not extras.

I work with clients in Brazil and worldwide, in English and Portuguese, remote-first with direct communication and clear deadlines. Based in Brazil (UTC-3) — a convenient overlap with both American and European working hours.
